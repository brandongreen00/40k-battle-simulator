# Deep visual review of the game interface — 2026-06-10

> **STATUS UPDATE (same day):** the fixes below were applied and verified in a follow-up session —
> **A1, B1–B6, C1–C5, D1–D3, plus E2/E4/E6/E7** (29/29 checks green in a scripted re-run of the
> full gameflow against the real app; 228 unit tests pass). The battle/sandbox split is a new
> `GameState.mode: 'sandbox' | 'match'` — rules guards apply to matches, the measuring board keeps
> its free behaviour. Still open from this review: C6 (base/terrain overlap is out of scope by
> design), E1 (per-unit import confirmation), E3 (move-budget rings), and the larger E5 fight-flow
> restructure. The findings are kept verbatim below as the record of what was observed.

**How this review was done.** A scripted headless-Chromium walkthrough (Playwright) drove the real
app end-to-end the way a player would, screenshotting every step: imported the owner's
"Rogue Trader's Army" 985-pt Imperialis Fleet list from the official-app text export, saved it,
opened it on the board, ran a full **New Battle** (roll-off → alternating deployment with
Reserves/Infiltrators → Leader attach → first-turn roll), then played a mirror match through all
five phases for three rounds (group moves, Advance, Deep-Strike arrival, shooting, charges, a
melee fight, Orders/Stratagems, battle-shock) and fast-forwarded to the round-5 end. Targeted
probes were then used to isolate root causes for everything that misbehaved. ~64 screenshots and
the dice logs were reviewed by eye.

**Headline:** the flow is genuinely playable end-to-end and the data layer is excellent — the
importer resolved all 10 units, wargear counts, Warlord and the enhancement with **zero warnings,
985/1000 pts, "Legal list"**, and the dice log is good enough to audit every roll. But there is
one **critical interaction bug** that wedges the board after the first confirmed move, plus a
cluster of missing guards that let a player (or a buggy AI) break a live game with one click.

## A. Critical — breaks the Movement phase for real users

### A1. Dragging units arms a text-selection that kills all board input
- **Symptom:** after one confirmed group move, drag-box selection permanently returns nothing
  (the rubber-band stays 0×0), and clicking tokens no longer selects. The Movement phase is
  effectively dead from the second activation onward.
- **Root cause (verified by native-event tracing):** nothing in the app sets `user-select: none`
  and the board's `pointerdown` handlers never `preventDefault()`. A press-drag that starts on a
  model token therefore *also* runs the browser's native text-selection — after the drag,
  `document.getSelection()` holds HUD/sidebar text (observed: *"Drag a selected model to move the
  group · Alt+drag…"* anchored in a sidebar `<h2>`). On the **next** board drag Chromium begins a
  native drag of that selection and fires **`pointercancel`** one move into the gesture
  (`pointerdown → gotpointercapture → 1 pointermove → pointercancel → lostpointercapture`), so
  `Board.handlePointerUp` never runs; the page can stay stuck in drag mode.
- **Fix (verified):** injecting `body { user-select: none }` made box-select work indefinitely
  across moves. Recommended:
  1. `user-select: none` on the app shell (`.layout`, `.lb-layout`) in `src/ui/styles.css` —
     measured text like the HUD/log can re-enable it locally if wanted;
  2. `e.preventDefault()` in `Board.handleTokenDown`/`handleBoardDown` for belt-and-braces;
  3. add `onPointerCancel={handlePointerUp}` to the `<svg>` so a cancelled gesture cleans up
     `groupDrag`/`selectBox`/capture instead of leaving stale state.

## B. High — missing guards that corrupt a live game

| # | Finding | Evidence | Fix pointer |
|---|---------|----------|-------------|
| B1 | **"Run Command" works in any phase and repeats** — each click is +1 CP and re-scores Primary (+5 VP per held objective). | Clicked twice in one Command phase: VP 5→10, CP 1→2. Clicked during *Shooting*: active side still gained CP. | `engine.runCommandPhase` has no `phase === 'Command'` / once-per-turn guard; gate it in the reducer (`state.ts` `RunCommandPhase`) or auto-run on entering Command and remove the button. |
| B2 | **Attack/Charge intents aren't phase-gated** — the Resolve Combat block renders in every phase and the engine accepts attacks during Command (rejected only "out of range"). | Probe: attack dispatched in Command phase → `Attack rejected: out of range (56.6" > 12")` — i.e. processed. | Add a phase check in the `Attack`/`Charge` reducer cases (per-phase *eligibility* inside Shooting/Fight already works well). |
| B3 | **Units can move twice** — after `EndMove` (even an Advance) the unit can be box-selected and given a fresh `BeginMove` with a full budget. | Advanced the Subductor Squad, confirmed, re-selected, clicked Move → "1 unit(s) moving · in coherency ✓". | `BeginMove` (`state.ts`) doesn't check `status.moved/advanced/fellBack`; filter already-moved units out (and grey them in the move buttons). |
| B4 | **Merged Leaders reappear as deployable → duplicate units** — `AttachLeader` removes the leader's unit instance, so `MeasuringBoard.isPlaced(entryKey)` turns false: the deploy list shows the leader with "+ Deploy" again and the remaining-counter grows back (observed 0→1). | B5 | **Sandbox controls bleed into a live battle** — once `BeginBattle` fires, the left rail reverts to the sandbox: "+ Place" any unit for either side (free reinforcements), "×" remove any unit (including the enemy's), "Clear all", "Spawn as". | Visible in every mid-battle screenshot. | The app can't currently distinguish "real match" from sandbox (`stage === 'battle'` for both). ## C. Fidelity gaps that decide games (visible in the dice logs)

- **C1. Every alive model fires the chosen weapon.** The Kill Team's single Infernus heavy bolter
  resolved as `9× Infernus heavy bolter: 27 attacks` (~3 marines slain per volley); in melee it
  fought with `9× Black Shield blades: 36 attacks` (8 Subductors slain in one activation) — a
  weapon the list doesn't even carry. The per-model loadout *is* on the roster
  (`wargearCounts`) — the engine just doesn't read it yet (known handoff item; this run shows how
  distorting it is in practice: it decides every combat).
- **C2. Casualties are allocated from the front of the model array** (`engine.ts` maps damage onto
  alive models in order), so kills strip whole front rows and the survivors are left out of 2"
  coherency. Consequences observed:
  - the shot-up Kill Team (11→4) was **permanently incoherent**, and because the Movement panel's
    Confirm is all-or-nothing over every moving unit, *one* such unit blocked an 8-unit group
    confirm (the panel doesn't name the offender — it just says "⚠ a unit is out of coherency");
  - engagement evaporates mid-fight: right after the Kill Team's melee kill, the Fight activation
    list flipped to "No units are in Engagement Range", so Consolidate guidance vanished.
  10e removes models of the owner's choice (normally the back); auto-removing from the rear (or
  re-packing survivors toward the unit centroid) would fix both, and the warning should name the
  unit.
- **C3. Charge pathing is stricter than the dice.** `Exaction Squad charges Callidus: 2D6=10" →
  failed (cannot reach all targets / would clip a non-target)` — a 10" roll failing at sub-10"
  range because the path search only translates the whole unit rigidly along one direction. Also
  dice-fail and path-fail share one message, so the player can't tell *why* a charge failed; log
  the needed distance and the blocking unit separately.
- **C4. Near-dead merged units offer dead weapons.** With only the Artemis model left alive in the
  merged Kill Team, the weapon picker still listed all Kill Team guns; resolving gave the cryptic
  `Attack rejected: no models`. Filter weapon options to datasheets with alive models.
- **C5. Deployment legality is centre-point only** (`deployment.ts` `inOwnZone`), so a base can
  overhang the zone edge — "wholly within" is not enforced. Minor, but cheap to fix with the base
  radius.
- **C6. No overlap rules at all**: units deploy/move through and on top of each other and terrain
  footprints (late-round blobs visibly interpenetrate). Known scope choice — recording it here
  because the screenshots make it look like a bug.

## D. UI defects (console-visible)

- **D1. `ListUnitCard.tsx:59` — `<option key={t.models}>`** duplicate keys (`1, 4, 10, 11`): the
  cost data has two rows per tier (normal + "AGENTS OF THE IMPERIUM Detachment"), so React drops
  options and the select displays the odd row — e.g. **"1 models — 110 pts (AGENTS OF THE IMPERIUM
  Detachment)"** for the Eversor. Filter the allied-detachment cost rows (or key by row index),
  fix the "1 model**s**" plural, and skip rendering a one-option select (fixed-size units already
  render plain "1 model" text — the two styles are inconsistent side by side).
- **D2. `GamePanel.tsx:323` — `<option key={w.name}>`** duplicate keys for merged units (both the
  Kill Team and Artemis have "Close combat weapon"): React warning, and the picker can't express
  *whose* weapon it is. Key by source datasheet + name, and label like "Close combat weapon
  (Artemis)".
- **D3. React StrictMode + impure reducer**: `reduce(state, intent, rng, ctx)` draws from the
  module-level RNG inside `useReducer`, and StrictMode double-invokes reducers in dev — every
  dispatch consumes the RNG twice, so in-app dice sequences can't be reproduced from the seed
  (tests use the same seed and *will* diverge from what the UI shows). Either draw dice before
  dispatch (put rolls *in* the intent) or accept and document the dev-only divergence.
- These three are the **only** console issues across the whole flow — otherwise the run was clean
  (no pageerrors, no failed requests).

## E. Smaller UX feedback, by gameflow step

1. **List Builder / import** — excellent. Nits: the import panel gives no unit-by-unit
   confirmation (a per-unit "resolved ✓" list would build trust); points show 985/1000 but there's
   no per-category breakdown (Characters/Battleline) like the app export.
2. **Roll-off & deployment** — dice, alternation, the red illegal ghost and the Infiltrators/Deep
   Strike tags all read clearly. Nits: the two "Attach Leaders" rows don't say **which side's**
   leader they refer to (two identical "Inquisitor Draxus" rows in a mirror match); "Finish
   deploying →" is enabled even with units still undeployed (it only requires >0 placed) — gate it
   on `remaining player+ai === 0` or confirm; objective markers under a deployed blob are easy to
   cover — consider drawing them above zone fills but below tokens.
3. **Movement** — budget clamps, Advance roll in the log, coherency triangles and the Confirm gate
   all work. Nits: no visual of the **move budget** (a range ring around the unit's origin would
   help a lot); the coherency warning should name the unit (panel + triangle tooltip); after
   `EndMove` the selection clears, which is right, but there's no "which units have already moved"
   indicator (greyed tokens or a ✓ in the unit list would prevent accidental double-moves once B3
   is fixed).
4. **Shooting** — eligibility labels ("(6 eligible)", "⚠ already shot this turn") are genuinely
   good. Nits: when a weapon has "(0 valid)" targets there's no *why* (range vs LoS vs Lone
   Operative — the engine knows; surface it); the attacker dropdown silently falls back to *all*
   units when none are eligible, which contradicts its own "(0 eligible)" label; weapon list mixes
   melee weapons into the Shooting phase picker (filter by phase).
5. **Charge / Fight** — multi-target checklist, 2D6 log, Fights First ordering and Pile
   In/Consolidate buttons are all there. Nits: C3's conflated failure message; the Fight block's
   "Pile In 3""/"Consolidate 3"" buttons act on the *Resolve Combat* attacker selection — a subtle
   coupling that took effort to discover (put the fight activation flow in one block with its own
   unit picker); alternating activation is shown but not enforced.
6. **Stratagems / Orders** — phase+detachment filtering works (Violent Acquisition,
   Close-Quarters Barrage, Counter-offensive appeared exactly when legal), reactive use on the
   opponent's turn deducts CP correctly, and At All Costs (Eliminate/Acquire) is surfaced in the
   Command phase. Nits: most stratagems still only spend CP + log (known); the generic
   "Orders & Stratagems → Issue to attacker" block applies any effect for free with no
   phase/side/CP logic — fine as a debug tool, but it sits between two real-rules blocks and looks
   official; consider labelling it "(debug)" or folding it away.
7. **Scoring / end** — +1 CP and Primary scoring land in the Command phase, battle-shock dice are
   shown with the Ld target, the game ends after round 5 with an "ended" badge and the log keeps
   the whole history. Nit: "Fight**ended**" renders unspaced in the turnbar; and nothing announces
   the *winner* — a final score card would be a satisfying capstone.
8. **Persistence** — saved lists survive reload (board pickers list "Rogue Trader's Army" ✓), but
   a mid-battle reload silently lands on an empty sandbox titled "Round 1 · player · Command",
   which reads like a fresh game rather than "your battle was lost" (save/load is a roadmap item;
   an explicit "no game in progress" state would avoid confusion meanwhile).

## F. What's working well (keep it)

- **Importer**: the exact app export round-trips: 10/10 units, model counts (incl. the 4-model
  Entourage and 11-model squads), thunder-hammer×4/shield counts, Warlord, Clandestine Operation,
  985 pts, legal — zero warnings.
- **Deployment** is the strongest screen: roll-off with real dice, alternating placement that
  follows the defender-first rule, zone-clamped ghosts, Reserves, leader merge with detach.
- **The dice log** is the app's best debugging/trust tool — every hit/wound/save/damage line with
  crits, Sustained, cover and per-model saves visible.
- **Reactive timing** (architecture rule #3) demonstrably works: a Core stratagem was bought by
  the non-active side during the opponent's Shooting phase.
- Eligibility/guard messaging where it exists ("already shot this turn", "out of coherency",
  "Reserves arrive from battle round 2", "✗ illegal here (outside your zone)") is consistently
  clear — extending that pattern to the gaps above is most of the UX work.

## Suggested fix order

1. A1 `user-select`/pointercancel (one CSS line + two small handlers — unblocks real play).
2. B1 Run-Command gating, B3 double-move guard, B2 phase gates (rules integrity).
3. B4 leader re-deploy, B5 sandbox bleed, B6 first-turn switcher (game-state integrity).
4. C2 casualty/coherency handling (biggest mid-game friction), then C1 per-model weapons (biggest
   fidelity win — the roster data is already there).
5. D1/D2 key fixes + the small UX nits in §E.
